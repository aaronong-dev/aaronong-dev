### Featured Project

#### 🎳 Bowling-Tournaments.com

A full-stack platform designed to modernize how bowling tournaments are discovered, managed, and followed.

The platform includes tournament discovery, live scoring, brackets, side pots, director profiles, notifications, and an AI-powered bowling assistant.

**Built with:** Next.js, TypeScript, React, Firebase, Firestore, Vercel, and Google Gemini
